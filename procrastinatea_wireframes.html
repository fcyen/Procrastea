ProcrastinaTea — Wireframes

Low-fidelity mobile screens mapped from the user flow. The whole product rests on one idea: you complete a habit, snap a photo as proof, and your friends see it and cheer you on — so proof-of-completion and the social feed are the spine of every screen below.
