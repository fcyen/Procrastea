Brand Guide · v1

Procrastea
Good habits are built together. Conquer procrasteanation, fill your teabag.

Procrastea is a social habit tracker where friends keep each other honest with photo proof. The brand should feel warm, encouraging and a little playful — a cup of tea with friends, never a clinical productivity app.

Construction

A matcha cup with a honey teabag tag and rising steam. The cup is the constant; the tag and steam may be dropped only below 20 px (favicon).

Clear space & size

Keep clear space equal to the cup's width on all sides. Minimum mark size 20 px; minimum lockup width 96 px.

Wordmark

Figtree Black. Always split the emphasis: Procras in charcoal, tea in matcha‑ink — the pun is the point.

✓ Use the mark on porcelain, matcha-tint or charcoal.
✓ Keep the tag honey and the cup matcha.
✓ Pair mark + wordmark for first/primary placements.
✕ Don't recolour the cup or stretch the mark.
✕ Don't place the light mark on a busy photo without a scrim.
✕ Don't set the wordmark in another typeface.

Personality & motion 05

Voice & tone

A
Encouraging, not preachy.

"Nice work — teabag filled +1." Never guilt-trip a missed day.

B
Warm & playful.

Lean into the tea pun and cosy metaphors. Short, human sentences.

C
Honest.

Proof and approval language is plain and trustworthy — the whole point is verifiable streaks.

Signature motions

Progress is expressed as brewing tea (from the brand brief):

1
Fill the teabag

Completing a habit drops a tea leaf into the bag — the core reward moment.

2
Pour hot water

Logging pours water into the cup; the group grid "fills up" together.

3
Steep

Streak milestones let the tea steep and deepen in colour.
