Brand Guide · v1

Procrastea
Good habits are built together. Conquer procrasteanation, fill your teabag.

Procrastea is a social habit tracker where friends keep each other honest with photo proof. The brand should feel warm, encouraging and a little playful — a cup of tea with friends, never a clinical productivity app.

Construction

Two tea leaves — a deeper leaf lower-left, a lighter leaf upper-right — whose negative space forms a checkmark: tea + "habit done" in one growing sprout.

Clear space & size

Keep clear space equal to the small leaf's height on all sides. The mark holds down to a 16 px favicon; minimum lockup width 96 px.

Wordmark

Figtree Black. Always split the emphasis: Procras in charcoal, tea in matcha‑ink — the pun is the point.

✓ Use the mark on porcelain, matcha-tint or charcoal.
✓ Keep both leaves green; let the check read through the gap.
✓ Pair mark + wordmark for first/primary placements.
✕ Don't recolour the leaves or stretch the mark.
✕ Don't fill the negative-space check with colour.
✕ Don't set the wordmark in another typeface.

Mascot — Teo 02

Teo is a teabag who fills up as you build a habit. Use him for onboarding, streaks, celebration and empty states — the tag shows the habit's name, and the tea level shows progress.

Teo · tag = habit
On charcoal

Anatomy & style

A soft, illustrated teabag: a translucent stapled bag, real tea leaves inside, a smiley face (dot eyes, open smile, blush) and a habit-name tag on a curled string.

✓ Swap the tag text per habit (Read, Run…).
✓ Let the leaves grow with the streak.
✕ Don't use Teo as the app icon — that's the leaf mark's job.

Progress — Fill your teabag 03

Every completed habit drops a tea leaf into the bag. The bag filling with leaves is the app's core reward device — on habit cards, streak celebrations and Teo himself.

Personality & motion 07

Voice & tone

A
Encouraging, not preachy.

"Nice work — teabag filled +1." Never guilt-trip a missed day.

B
Warm & playful.

Lean into the tea pun and cosy metaphors. Short, human sentences.

C
Honest.

Proof and approval language is plain and trustworthy — the whole point is verifiable streaks.

Signature motions

Progress is expressed as brewing tea (from the brand brief):

1
Fill the teabag

Completing a habit drops a tea leaf into the bag — the core reward moment.

2
Pour hot water

Logging pours water into the cup; the group grid "fills up" together.

3
Steep

Streak milestones let the tea steep and deepen in colour.
