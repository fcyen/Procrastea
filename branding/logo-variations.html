Four ways to wear the tea

The current guide ships direction 00. Below are three more logo concepts on the same Matcha/Honey/Charcoal palette and Figtree wordmark — pick one to carry into the high-fidelity screens (or mix, e.g. a mascot for onboarding + a simple mark for the app icon).

Matcha cup + teabag tag

A tidy teacup with a honey tag and steam. Neutral and grown-up; reads as "tea + habit" without a character. This is what's in brand-guide.html today.

Tea leaf = checkmark

A single tea leaf whose vein is a check — "habit done" and "tea" in one mark. The most scalable and app-icon-friendly option; confident and modern, ages well, works in one colour.

Fill the cup

A cup with a rising tea level and a floating leaf — the logo literally embodies "fill your teabag / build the habit." The fill line can animate up as a progress device across the app. Distinctive and ownable.
