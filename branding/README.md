# Procrastea — branding guide (step 7)

Open **`brand-guide.html`** for the full guide (logo, colour, typeface, UI components,
personality & motion).

```
branding/
├─ brand-guide.html      the full visual brand guide (uses direction 00)
├─ logo-variations.html  4 logo directions to choose from (00 + A/B/C)
├─ colors.yml            colour palette with roles, tints and semantic mapping
└─ logo-mark.svg         the direction-00 teacup + teabag mark (standalone)
```

**Logo directions** (see `logo-variations.html`): 00 matcha cup + teabag tag (shipped),
A "Teo" teabag mascot (playful), B tea-leaf-as-checkmark (minimal, my pick for the app
icon), C fill-the-cup (conceptual/progress). Awaiting a pick to lock into the hi-fi screens.

Grounded in `raw files/` — `branding.yml` (tagline, Figtree, tea motions),
`color_palette.png` (palette) and `teabag.png` (mascot).

**At a glance**
- **Logo** — a matcha cup with a honey teabag tag and steam; wordmark *Procras**tea*** in
  Figtree Black with "tea" in matcha-ink. Works as mark, wordmark, lockup, and on charcoal.
- **Colour** — Matcha `#A0D286` (primary), Charcoal `#20252D` (ink), Honey `#F6C343`
  (accent/streaks), Steep `#2E5B9A` (links/info), Porcelain `#FAFBFC` (paper); Matcha-ink
  `#4C7D33` for readable green text/success.
- **Typeface** — Figtree, 400–900, with a Display→Caption scale.
- **Components** — buttons, inputs, chips, toggles/checks/radios, pills, stat tiles, tab bar,
  avatars, icons — the branded versions of the wireframe atoms.
