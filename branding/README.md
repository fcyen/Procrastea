# Procrastea — branding guide (step 7)

Open **`brand-guide.html`** for the full guide (logo, mascot, progress, colour, typeface,
UI components, personality & motion).

```
branding/
├─ brand-guide.html      the full visual brand guide
├─ logo-variations.html  the logo directions that were explored (00 + A/B/C)
├─ colors.yml            colour palette with roles, tints and semantic mapping
├─ logo-mark.svg         the primary two-leaf / checkmark mark (standalone)
└─ teabag.svg            the illustrated teabag (label = habit; fills with leaves)
```

**Locked direction:** **B — two-leaf sprout with a checkmark in the negative space** is the
primary logo / app icon (refined from the flat first draft into two overlapping leaves), and
**A — "Teo" the teabag mascot** is kept for onboarding, streaks, celebration and empty states.
See `logo-variations.html` for the concepts that were compared.

Grounded in `raw files/` — `branding.yml` (tagline, Figtree, tea motions),
`color_palette.png` (palette) and `teabag.png` (mascot).

**At a glance**
- **Logo** — two tea leaves whose negative space forms a check ("tea" + "habit done"); wordmark
  *Procras**tea*** in Figtree Black with "tea" in matcha-ink. Works as mark, wordmark, lockup, on charcoal, to favicon size.
- **Mascot (Teo)** — a soft, illustrated **translucent teabag** with real tea leaves and a smiley face;
  the tag shows the **habit's name** and the leaves grow with progress.
- **Progress** — "fill your teabag": each completed habit drops a leaf in; shown as 0→100% fill states.
- **Colour** — Matcha `#A0D286` (primary), Charcoal `#20252D` (ink), Honey `#F6C343`
  (accent/streaks), Steep `#2E5B9A` (links/info), Porcelain `#FAFBFC` (paper); Matcha-ink
  `#4C7D33` for readable green text/success.
- **Typeface** — Figtree, 400–900, with a Display→Caption scale.
- **Components** — buttons, inputs, chips, toggles/checks/radios, pills, stat tiles, tab bar,
  avatars, icons — the branded versions of the wireframe atoms.
