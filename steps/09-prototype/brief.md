# Step 9 — Prototype

## Goal
Turn the high-fidelity wireframes into an interactive prototype that a test
user can click through, covering the full user flow.

## Inputs
- High-fidelity wireframes (step 8 deliverable)
- `raw files/User Flow.png`

## Acceptance criteria
An interactive prototype (HTML/CSS/JS) covering the user flow, ready to put
in front of users for step 10 (Testing).
