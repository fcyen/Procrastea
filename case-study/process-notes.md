**Thesis:** humans own the thinking that needs intent (structure, journey, brand); AI
accelerates the breadth and the build.

## The process, step by step

1. **Brainstorm in FigJam** — _Human · FigJam_
   - Open-ended sticky-note ideation — kept wide on purpose to make room for breadth.
   - Talked through the apps we use daily and what's trending.
   - Landed on a Setlog-inspired social habit tracker.

2. **Market scan** — _AI-assisted_
   - With a broad direction set, asked AI to surface similar apps.
   - Reviewed the list and narrowed to four competitors — direct, or adjacent with proven success (>100k downloads).

3. **In-depth competitor analysis** — _Human + AI_
   - Gave AI research directions per competitor: strengths, gaps, est. DAU, time in market.
   - Downloaded and used the apps ourselves; noted first-use impressions.
   - Had AI fold our notes into the final analysis table.

4. **Framing the problem** — _Human_
   - Extracted user pain points from the analysis.
   - Wrote the problem statement, proposed solution and value proposition.

5. **User personas** — _AI draft_
   - Asked AI to generate 3 personas from the research and framing.

6. **Information architecture** — _By hand · FigJam_
   - Reviewed the personas, then built the IA as flowcharts by hand.
   - Kept it manual on purpose — intentional about how information is structured.

7. **User journey** — _By hand · FigJam_
   - Plotted the journey by hand to force thinking through the whole flow.
   - Made significant IA changes along the way — designing a proper experience and closing gaps we saw in existing habit trackers.

8. **Wireframes** — _AI + human review_
   - AI generated wireframes from the IA and user journey.
   - Reviewed the screens; sketched over the ones needing significant layout changes.

9. **Design guide** — _By hand_
   - Built a mood board; chose palette and typography for the target audience — working adults.
   - Aimed for trust and professionalism, while keeping fun elements so habit tracking stays enjoyable.

10. **High-fidelity prototype** — _AI build_
    - Built the high-fidelity, clickable prototype from the design guide.

## Reflection & what's next

11. **What didn't work — the friction** — _Honest reflection_
    - Branding first went out on an off-brand coral accent before we reset to the matcha palette.
    - AI built the group screen as a vertical feed; we redirected to the Setlog-style 2×4 proof grid.
    - The logo's first leaf-check was too flat — took reference art and a few passes to reach the two-leaf mark and the cozy teabag.
    - A flattened FigJam PDF export was too low-res to transcribe (personas) — needed higher-res, per-frame exports.
    - Pattern: AI moves fast but guesses; tight, frequent review caught the drift early.

12. **Testing, refine & takeaways** — _Human-led · next_
    - Test: run the prototype with target users (working adults); capture friction and points of confusion.
    - Refine: address feedback — tighten the log → approve flow and thread the illustrated teabag through the screens.
    - Takeaway: humans own the intent (structure, journey, brand); AI owns the breadth and the build.
    - Keep a human firmly in the loop at the decision points — direction, taste, and reference-setting.
