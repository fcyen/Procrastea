04
User Personas
AI-generated

"The Resolution Repeater" (primary)

Junior marketing associate. She's downloaded four habit trackers, and each died within three weeks — she'd skip a day, feel guilty, then avoid the app entirely. Her goal is the gym 4×/week and reading before bed; her frustration is that "nobody knows when I quit, so quitting is free."

Design implication: make skipping visible to friends without making it humiliating.

Maya, 29

"The Accountability Buddies" (primary)

They already text each other "did you run today?" They want a private two-person pact with proof, because they both know a text saying "yep, ran" is easy to fake.

Design implication: the app must work brilliantly at group size 2, not just 12 — most real accountability happens in pairs.

Dan & Chris, long-distance friends

"The Group Challenge Organizer" (secondary)

Runs a "75 Hard"-style challenge with seven coworkers every January, currently managed through a group chat full of unverifiable claims. She's the monetization target: she'll pay for a group plan, custom rules, and challenge templates.

Design implication: group creation and invite flow must be frictionless.

Joanne, 32
