Designing Procrastea with an AI collaborator

A case study on running a full UX process — research to prototype — with Claude as a design partner: what it did well, where a human had to steer, and how the two split the work.

🚧 This case study hasn't been written yet. The outline below is a placeholder skeleton — each slide will be filled in with narrative, artefacts and before/after screenshots.

Proposed slide outline

01

The brief & the setup

What Procrastea is, why "social proof + habits", and how the human + AI workflow was set up (a repo, a LOOP.md process, Claude Code on the web).

02

Research & synthesis

Competitor teardown (Setlog, BeReal, Folksable, HabitShare), pain points and personas.

AI did: transcribed the FigJam board, structured the comparison & personas. Human did: the actual research and insight.
03

IA & user flow

Turning the flow into Mermaid diagrams and reconciling them with the screens.

AI did: converted diagrams, cross-checked flow ↔ wireframes.
04

Wireframes

Splitting a monolith into 19 per-screen files; the photo-first log flow; the setlog-style group grid with proof approval.

AI did: built & iterated screens from sketches. Human did: reviewed and redirected (grid vs feed, approval rules).
05

Branding

From a coral placeholder to the matcha palette, the two-leaf logo, Teo the teabag mascot and the "fill your teabag" progress device — including logo exploration.

AI did: generated logo directions & the guide. Human did: picked B, supplied reference art, pushed for the cozy teabag.
06

Hi-fi & prototype

Re-skinning the screens in-brand and wiring the clickable prototype.

AI did: built the interactive prototype.
07

What worked, what didn't

Honest reflection: speed and breadth from AI, vs. taste, judgement and reference-setting from the human. Where AI got it wrong and how the loop caught it.

08

Takeaways

A repeatable pattern for AI-assisted design, and when to keep a human firmly in the loop.
