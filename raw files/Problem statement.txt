Problem
People struggle to stay consistent with habits because traditional habit trackers feel lonely and accountability feels like pressure.

Solution
A social habit tracker where friends encourage each other by sharing proof of completed habits.

Value proposition
A habit tracker that reduces the friction of logging and leverages on the power of social community to motivate user to stay on track

User Pain Points
- Data logging fatigue
- The Guilt of Broken Streaks
- The Loneliness of Self-Improvement
- Lack of Verifiable Proof
