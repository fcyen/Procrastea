# 2 · Design principles

> The board has no standalone "design principles" artifact. These are **derived** from the
> research, pain points and personas (`01-ux-research.md`, `04-personas.md`) — replace or
> confirm if the team has an explicit version.

1. **Proof over self-report.** Every completion is a photo. This is the trust gap HabitShare
   never closed and the reason "quitting is free" for Maya — verified proof makes streaks mean something.

2. **Supportive accountability, not shame.** Skipping should be *visible to friends* but never
   humiliating. Approval and reactions are encouragement, not judgement.

3. **Log in seconds.** Logging fatigue killed Maya's last four apps. Photo-first capture; pick
   the habit *after* the shot; never make logging feel like data entry.

4. **Works brilliantly at group size 2.** Most real accountability is a pair (Dan & Chris).
   One trusted person's approval outweighs many likes — design for pairs first, groups second.

5. **Frictionless groups.** Invite codes / QR, fast group creation, light admin controls
   (e.g. daily window) so an organizer like Priya can spin up a challenge in minutes.

6. **Momentum you can see.** Borrow Setlog's "fill the grid" pull and express it as
   Procrastea's **"fill your teabag"** metaphor — a warm, rewarding sense of progress.
